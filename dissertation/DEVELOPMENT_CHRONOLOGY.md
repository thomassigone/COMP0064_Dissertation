# Development chronology A–U

Paths are relative to `android-vulnerability-agent/`. This is an evidence/navigation table, not chapter prose. Stage objectives A–S are checked against `README.md`, linked code/docs and `git log --reverse`; T/U against frozen evidence. “Survived” means retained concept or code, not that an early experiment is part of final scoring.
